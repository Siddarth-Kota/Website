About Me

A passionate computer engineer with a focus on digital design and verification.

GT
Georgia Institute of Technology B.S. Computer Engineering · May 2028

Hi, my name is Siddarth Kota. I am currently a junior at Georgia Tech pursuing a B.S. in Computer Engineering, with graduation expected in May 2028. I’m interested in silicon design and verification. During college, I have explored these areas through coursework, projects, clubs, research, and internships.

I interned at AMD on the platform design engineering team, focusing on designing and verifying next-generation server boards supporting the EPYC processor line. I have also gotten involved in research with Professor Tushar Krishna at Synergy Lab. There, I focus on developing simulation software called SCALE-Sim-EVA to analyze and design custom AI accelerator architectures.

In digital design, I have designed and verified my own custom RISC-V processor and am currently working on improving its performance and implementing it on an FPGA. Similarly, I’m part of SiliconJackets’ digital design team, focusing on developing RTL for a RISC-V core for its annual tapeouts.

I’m currently seeking digital design and verification internships for summer 2027.
