Georgia Institute of Technology B.S. Computer Engineering · May 2028 4.00 GPA

I’m Siddarth Kota, a Computer Engineering student at Georgia Tech. My work spans digital design, verification, hardware validation, and accelerator modeling. What connects those areas is the journey from an architectural idea to precise logic, then to a system that behaves as intended under real constraints.

My projects let me explore that journey from different directions. Designing a RISC-V pipeline means thinking about dependencies and control flow. Building an audio analyzer connects data capture, numerical processing, and a visible output. A lab sensor brings embedded computing and board design into a practical setting.

I approach engineering through three questions: what should this system do, how does the implementation behave, and what evidence supports that behavior? Testbenches, waveforms, timing reports, and measurements each help answer a different part. I want to keep getting better at connecting them.

Beyond my technical work, I’m an Eagle Scout, a HackATL finalist with the Alta Mind AI companion app, and a second-place finisher in the Sierra Circuits PCB Design Competition. Those experiences sit alongside research, coursework, and collaborative hardware projects in the perspective I bring to engineering.
